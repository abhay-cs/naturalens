Clear space

Minimum clear space on all four sides equals the height of the owl's eye — 0.5× the mark height. Nothing crosses it: no type, no rules, no image edges.

24px is the floor. Below 40px, increase stroke to 2.5 so the pupils stay open. Never reproduce the mark smaller than 24px or with the wordmark below 16px.

full · eyes, CTA

The mark holds two geometries: the perfect circle of the eye and the sharp point of the tuft. Full-pill belongs to the single primary action on a screen — the one thing that behaves like a lens. Everything else is rectangular at 0–2px, so the pill stays rare enough to read as an instruction.

06

Iconography

32 grid · 2px stroke · round caps

Icons are drawn on the same 32 grid as the mark, at 2px stroke with round caps and joins, so an icon set beside the owl reads at one optical weight. Fills are solid black only — no two-tone, no outline-plus-tint, no duotone. When a shape needs emphasis, fill it; when it needs recession, leave it hollow.

07

Motion

Entrance

Fade with 8px rise. 480ms, cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1). One stagger of 60ms, never more.

State change

220ms on color and border only. No scale on hover, no bounce, no spring overshoot.

Recognition

The result arrives as one still moment: the frame holds, the name fades in, nothing else moves for 600ms.

Reduced motion

All entrances collapse to opacity. Nothing in the system depends on movement to be understood.

08

Do / Don't

Do
Let the mark carry the hero at full scale.
Give one screen one primary action.
Use full-bleed black or white for atmosphere.
Separate with hairlines and space, not boxes.
Set metadata in tracked caps, prose in Archivo, voice in Outfit.
Don't
Purple or any gradient background.
A card grid inside the hero.
Floating badges or chips over media.
Rainbow pills and multi-tone icons.
Two CTAs of equal weight side by side.
